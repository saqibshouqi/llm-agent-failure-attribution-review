Overview

This systematic review synthesizes 68 studies on failure attribution and recovery for long-horizon large language model agents. It develops a six-dimensional taxonomy, maps gaps across detection, localization, rollback, state and context restoration, and evaluation, and proposes an attribution-guided recovery loop with a Break-Even Attribution Accuracy construct. The proposed framework is conceptual and awaits empirical validation.
